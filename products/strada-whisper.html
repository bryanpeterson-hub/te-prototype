STRADA Whisper High Speed Backplane Connectors

Overview Products Related Materials

Scalable Backplane Solutions to 112 Gbps PAM-4

The STRADA Whisper backplane family was designed with your need for high-performing, high-bandwidth systems in mind. Its revolutionary design transfers data up to 112 Gbps—allowing you to achieve efficient future system upgrades without costly backplane or midplane redesigns.

Shop Backplane Connectors (9) Connect With STRADA Whisper Expert →

STRADA Whisper connectors are differentiated by folded signal pins surrounded by strong, protective C-shaped shields, which make the product family one of the most robust offerings in the market. Additionally, the connector footprint keeps crosstalk down and the overall connector design uses latest eye-of-needle (EON) technology.

The STRADA Whisper product family operates with extremely low noise, low insertion loss and little to no skew—all of which provide design flexibility and high design margin.

Featured Product: STRADA Whisper Absolute for 112G Backplane

Enabling Next-Gen Architectures

TE's STRADA Whisper Absolute backplane connectors provide a cost-effective solution for 112G PAM 4 architectures, with an improved design footprint that allows for improved performance without sacrificing customer routing capabilities. With excellent cross talk and insertion loss performance, and a backwards compatible interface reducing overall engineering needs when upgrading from 56G to 112G operations – TE is your partner for 112G upgrades.

Product Benefits

  • Enables 112G network architectures through excellent performance in key requirements for cross talk, return Loss, and insertion loss.
  • Designed for 112G PAM-4 backplane applications with improved performance without sacrificing routing optionality.
  • Cost effective upgrade solution due to fully backwards-compatible interface from 56G.
Brochure STRADA Whisper Absolute Connectors (English)

TE Connectivity's (TE) STRADA Whisper Absolute backplane connectors enable 112G network architectures. The new design minimizes skew with noise isolation and 360 degree grounding capabilities. These products are manufactured with a fully automated assembly process, helping reduce operating cycle times, operating cost and enhance machine productivity. The backward-compatible interface enables a reduction in mechanical design churn when the focus is to only upgrade internal components.

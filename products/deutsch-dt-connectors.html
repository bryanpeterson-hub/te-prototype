DEUTSCH DT Connectors

Heavy-duty sealed connectors for harsh automotive environments. Ideal for EV battery and charging applications. DEUTSCH DT connectors deliver reliable performance in demanding conditions with IP67 sealing.

Key Features

  • IP67 environmental sealing
  • 600V voltage rating
  • 13A current rating
  • Operating temperature: -40°C to +125°C
  • Copper alloy contacts for reliability
  • Designed for EV battery and charging systems

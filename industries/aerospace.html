Mini-ETH Single Pair Ethernet (SPE) System for Aircraft Design

Single Pair Ethernet (SPE) enables Ethernet transmission with just two wires, reducing weight and complexity. TE's Mini-ETH system meets ARINC 854 standards for next-generation aircraft connectivity.

MiniMRP Avionics Packaging

Distributed avionics systems reduce weight and improve reliability. The Mini Modular Rack Principle (MiniMRP) and ARINC 836A standards enable flexible, scalable aircraft architectures.

Empowering Engineers for Tech Innovation

TE Connectivity helps engineers design and build the technologies transforming how people live, work, and connect. As a go-to connectivity partner for innovation leaders and technology entrepreneurs, we work on solving tomorrow's toughest technology challenges today. Our global team of regionally located experts – which includes 10,000 electrical and mechanical engineers working alongside customers in approximately 130 countries – develops and manufactures connectivity solutions enabling technological innovation for a safer, sustainable, productive, and connected world.

Every day, our teams are working to advance the core systems of complex machines, with rugged, robust solutions for harsh-environment reliability for enabling the integration of "Sense and Connect" technologies. Our broad portfolio of electrical parts and electronic components include different types of connectors and sensors optimally engineered to reliably connect, protect, and optimize the flow of data, power, and signal – in electric vehicles and aircraft, in digital factories and smart homes, and in life-saving medical devices, energy-efficient utility networks, and the global communications infrastructure, from IOT devices to AI systems.

Building Effective Recommenders in Salesforce

An overview of how to configure and deploy recommendation strategies using Salesforce Personalization.

  • Preparation & validation: simulate strategies in a "white box" environment and test efficacy with internal or power users before going live.
  • Configuring recommenders: choose data sources (real-time graph) and define target items — products, families, categories, or content.
  • Recommendation logic: rule-based "wisdom of the crowd" or objective-based one-to-one ML decisioning to drive specific behaviors.
  • Refining results: apply inclusion/exclusion criteria across decision context, static campaign rules, and profile data graph.

Your question How exactly are affinity scores and recommendation models configured, weighted, and optimized?

Understanding Salesforce Calculated Affinities

An overview of calculated affinities — a tool that analyzes how visitors engage with digital content by assigning numerical interest scores to products or metadata.

  • Data space & look-back window: choose global or business-unit affinities and set a relevant timeframe (e.g. 60 days) tied to current buying cycles.
  • Item identification: select the Data Cloud objects and metadata (product name, category) to evaluate.
  • Engagement events: assign positive or negative sentiment strengths to actions like browsing, purchasing, or inquiries.
  • Actioning data: scores write back to the profile for targeted segments, auditing in Data Explorer, and automated one-to-one recommendations.

Streamlining Lead Conversion in Salesforce

A demonstration of how to convert leads into accounts, contacts, and opportunities using out-of-the-box Salesforce functionality.

  • One-step conversion: automatically create a new account, contact, and opportunity when converting a lead.
  • Avoid duplicates: attach the lead to an existing account, contact, or opportunity — or skip opportunity creation entirely.
  • Data carries over: all lead information transfers to the new records, and prior lead activity is preserved on the account.
  • Automation with control: define clear triggers and keep a human in the loop to avoid bloating the funnel or skewing pipeline data.
